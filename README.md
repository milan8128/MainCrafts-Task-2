# Data Analytics & Business Intelligence – Task 2

## Advanced Data Analysis, SQL Joins & Business KPI Dashboard

This project is part of the MainCrafts Data Analytics & Business Intelligence Task 2.

The project focuses on analyzing Superstore sales data using SQL, Excel and Power BI to generate business insights and recommendations.

## Business Insights

1. Technology is the highest-selling category, generating approximately 0.83M in sales.
2. The West region has the highest sales at approximately 710.22K.
3. The Consumer segment contributes the highest sales, approximately 1.15M (50.76%).
4. Phones are the top-selling sub-category with approximately 0.33M in sales.
5. Sales vary throughout the year, with the highest sales occurring around September.

## Business Recommendations

1. Increase marketing and promotions for the Technology category.
2. Strengthen the West region and identify further growth opportunities.
3. Create targeted offers for Consumer customers.
4. Promote high-performing sub-categories such as Phones and Chairs.
5. Use promotional campaigns during weaker sales months.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how SQL, Excel and Power BI can be used together to analyze sales data, calculate KPIs, create an interactive dashboard, identify business trends and provide actionable business recommendations.
